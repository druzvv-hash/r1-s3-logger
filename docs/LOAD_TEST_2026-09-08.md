# Load acceptance — 3 A / 3 V, 2026-09-08

PANEL v0.17 is deployed and passed the following USB bench checks. The owner
reported a 3 A / 3 V load. These are stability and transport results, not an
independent reference calibration. Shunt, gains, offsets and ADC settings were
unchanged. Rate changes used Apply only; the complete original configuration was
restored to 50 Hz afterward, with saved generation 3. No Save or clock adjustment
was issued.

## Acquisition

The shared I2C bus ran at 100 kHz with OLED enabled. Live rows were collected by
sequence; delivered frequency is calculated from their monotonic timestamps.

| Requested Hz | Window | Collected samples | Delivered Hz | Mean V | Mean A | Mean W |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10 | 10 s | 99 | 10.0000 | 2.99718 | 3.00076 | 8.99382 |
| 50 | 10 s | 500 | 50.0002 | 2.99779 | 2.99891 | 8.99012 |
| 100 | 30 s | 3000 | 100.0004 | 2.99877 | 2.99953 | 8.99489 |

All three windows had zero invalid samples, missed periods, I2C errors, preview
producer drops, sequence gaps and history overruns. The 10 Hz count reflects the
capture boundary, not a missing sequence. Explicit Apply transitions are outside
the acceptance windows and retain their maintenance gap markers.

At 100 Hz, read-completion intervals ranged from 8.917 to 11.099 ms, averaging
9.99996 ms. Current ranged from 2.94583 to 3.05208 A (standard deviation 0.01568 A);
voltage ranged from 2.99277 to 3.00391 V (standard deviation 0.00168 V). INA228
temperature averaged 23.02 degrees C. The test cannot separate source ripple,
wiring noise and measurement noise. No new filtering or calibration was applied.

## Simultaneous acquisition and SD downloads

A further 60-second phase kept acquisition at 100 Hz while repeatedly downloading
the existing 4096-byte `/eeprom_before_0000.bin` file through the USB bridge.

- 40 completed downloads, 163,840 bytes total; mean transfer time 0.878 s.
- Each complete file matched SHA-256
  `78c5017e9cac1ce1807b838a3c168c10e8ab1776354b7e68abdaaa1b10cd8545`.
  The bridge also validates session, offset, size and per-block CRC32.
- Firmware counters advanced by 6069 valid samples at an observed 100.0 Hz,
  with zero invalid samples, missed periods, I2C errors, preview producer drops
  or maintenance pauses. Counter endpoints include request overhead.
- No reboot occurred. Minimum observed free internal heap was 134,184 bytes.
- Periodic Live tail snapshots contained 1920 points, averaging 3.00052 V,
  2.99909 A and 8.99883 W. These are sampled snapshots, not a complete archive
  of all 6069 acquired samples.
- State age relative to the fresh sample clock averaged 432 ms, maximum 708 ms.

After restoring 50 Hz, the real browser displayed numeric live readings and
rendered at 59.67 FPS in 60 FPS mode without JavaScript errors. Its reported
connection stayed online; the desktop screenshot was reviewed. This browser
acceptance occurred after the download phase, not concurrently with it.

## Scheduling fix found by the test

On v0.16, a healthy 100 Hz acquisition could leave the published panel state
unchanged for several seconds. State publication was scheduled after optional
OLED transfers, which consumed the remaining acquisition slack. v0.17 publishes
the due snapshot before optional RTC/OLED work, retaining the existing slack
check and acquisition priority. The final test showed regular publication at
approximately 510 ms and no acquisition errors.

An earlier attempt to archive every Live row while downloading repeatedly fell
behind on the shared 115200-baud UART. Hardware acquisition counters still showed
no missed or invalid samples. The 2048-point preview history is finite and cannot
replace the planned PSRAM FIFO-to-SD recorder. The final concurrent test therefore
checks hardware counters, sampled live tails and complete downloaded file hashes;
it does not claim lossless simultaneous USB sample archival.

## Build, deployment and limits

Normal and service v0.17 builds pass; only normal was uploaded. Normal static RAM
is 70,832 bytes and flash usage 937,321 bytes. Upload needed a retry at 57600 baud;
the successful write verified every flash hash. The project default remains
115200 baud. Intermittent upload reliability is still a separate open issue.

These short tests cover USB download of an existing small file. Long recordings,
SD writes during acquisition, card removal, native AP transfer and independent
calibration remain unverified. Production session recording is the next P5 task.

Private raw evidence is kept under ignored `data/load-tests/20260908-102618/`:
`report.json`, per-rate Live rows and snapshots, transfer snapshots, `ui.json`
and `load-ui.png`. Earlier v0.16 attempts are in sibling directories ending
`101422` and `101711`. EEPROM contents and local bridge credentials are not
included in this public report.
